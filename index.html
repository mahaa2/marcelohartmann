About me

I am an Academic Reseach Fellow by the Reseach Council of Finland (RCF) at the Department of Computer Science, University of Helsinki. Prior to that I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the same institution, working alonside the Finnish Center for Artificial Intelligence where I independently developed methods of prior predictive elicitation and computationally efficient inference methods associate with notions of on information geometry. I earned my PhD in Statistics with distinction from the University of Helsinki, in that thesis I developed multi-source statistical models based on multivariate Gaussian processes (GPs), with new, stable and fast geometric optimisation algorithms for GPs. I also hold a master degree in Statistics by the Interinstitutional Graduate Program in Statistics UFSCar-USP (Brazil), a partnership between Department of Statistics at the Federal University of São Carlos (UFSCar) and the Department of Applied Mathematics and Statistics at the Institute of Mathematical and Computer Sciences of the University of São Paulo (ICMC-USP).
